Yusaku Nakai, Shoi Nagaoka, Kodai Fujita, Haruhisa Ichikawa, Yuusuke Kawakita, "Control of Battery Usage in Smartphones Based on a Regression Model," 2025 IEEE International Conference on Consumer Electronics - Taiwan (ICCE-TW), Kaohsiung, Taiwan, Jul. 2025.
