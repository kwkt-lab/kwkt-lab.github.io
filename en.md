<h1>Kawakita Lab (IoT Systems Lab)</h1>

Department of Information and Computer Sciences, Faculty of Information Technology, Kanagawa Institute of Technology

I study IoT systems that integrate power, communication, sensing and control, and I validate theory through real implementations. My current topics are:

1. **Device-free wireless sensing** that estimates human activity and spatial conditions (crowding, rainfall) from Wi-Fi CSI and RSSI, combined with positioning-free signal-source clustering.
2. **Battery-free backscatter communication** that extends RFID to stream many sensor signals at the same time while keeping compatibility with the RFID standard.
3. **An Internet-style power platform** that connects small generation and storage units, including routing in hub-based DC grids and faster control using quantum annealing.

I am also applying these technologies to healthcare, such as urine-volume sensing for end-of-life care.
